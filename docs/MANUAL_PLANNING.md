# Manual arrangements for the admin

After loading the three Excel files, choose **Edit routes** to adjust your generated plan, or **Arrange manually** to start without the automatic planner. To revise a plan already shared with the team after refreshing the browser, open **Private Excel files · admin only**, choose **Load saved files**, then **Edit published routes**. The published names must match the loaded roster.

1. Choose a practice date. Add or remove cars and select each driver. Selecting a driver records that they can drive that day, including a replacement who was not available in the original sheet. Set the actual seats, including the driver (maximum five).
2. In **Crew and travel**, move riders between cars, choose **Not attending** for cancellations, or choose **Uber**, **Own travel**, or **Other arranged transport**. Replacing or removing a driver leaves that person unassigned until you choose their travel or attendance.
3. Use the arrows to set pickup order. Open **Pickup details** to change that day's pickup / driver start or ready time. Adjacent riders at the same pickup share one stop. Existing roster members who were absent can be added by assigning them to a car. Add a new person to Members and reupload before arranging them.
4. Leave **Leave at** blank to calculate departure, or enter a specific time. A specified departure stays fixed. For an unusually early start, update the affected people’s ready times as well. You can also change the arrival time for this plan. All times are Eastern Time, shown to the minute.
5. Choose **Generate updated plan**. TomTom recalculates the selected routes and road maps while preserving your driver assignments and pickup order. It checks capacity, complete coverage, readiness and arrival times. It does not run the automatic weekly search or request a full travel matrix.
6. Review the result, then choose **Publish to team / Update published plan** or **Download Excel**. The published plan remains unchanged until you publish the revision. Team members cannot open the editor or change routes.

**Cancel edits** keeps the previous result. Routing failures retain the draft so it can be corrected or cancelled. Unassigned people, duplicate assignments, overloaded cars and impossible times must be resolved before generation. Uber groups use one pickup per group, up to four riders, and require a separate booking.

Manual changes apply to the resulting plan, not to the uploaded Excel or Google Sheets. **Replan from files** replaces those changes using the original inputs. Unsaved editor changes are kept only in the current browser tab; published arrangements can be reopened as described above. Historical comparisons retain their existing publication restriction.

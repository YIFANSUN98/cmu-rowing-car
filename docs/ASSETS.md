# Image sources

`public/branding/cmu-rowing-logo.png` is the unmodified 215 × 215 PNG crest published by the club's [@cmurowing social profile](https://linktr.ee/tartancrew), retrieved September 24, 2026. That profile links to the [official Tartan Crew website](https://tartancrew.wixsite.com/tartancrew) and the [@cmurowing Instagram account](https://www.instagram.com/cmurowing/).

Source image: <https://ugc.production.linktr.ee/Kl7yNtKDSRakusgnXYKW_Zt9QwoeVioAy3jkE>. The site serves its own copy in the header, closing brand and browser-tab icon; loading the planner does not contact social-media services. The original PNG is unchanged. The shared `ClubBrand` component displays the crest proportionately at a larger size, using CSS inversion and screen blending to present white artwork on CMU red without a white square. This is a display treatment, not a new or redrawn club logo. The crest remains the property of its respective owner.

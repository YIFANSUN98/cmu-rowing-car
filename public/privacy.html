Privacy

Launch uses two shared access keys. The admin key permits uploads, planning, publishing and password changes. The team key permits viewing the published routes and downloading the plan. There are no individual accounts, account database, analytics or automatic messages.

Excel files are first read and validated in your browser. Plan week or Save Excel files saves the original workbooks in the application's private storage, where only admins can retrieve them. Publishing saves selected names, pickup addresses, coordinates, road paths and times for the team view. Unused roster entries, attendance grids and original files are not sent to team users. The server retains the latest input set and published plan until replaced or removed.

Signing in sets an HttpOnly session cookie. Keys are stored as salted password hashes on the server; access keys and spreadsheets are not stored in localStorage. Sessions expire, are revoked on logout, and stop authorizing requests when the matching key changes. The page checks sessions every 30 seconds while visible and when you return to the tab. Shared keys cannot identify individual members. Anyone with a team key can copy or download the published route information.

The admin page fetches public Pittsburgh weather observations from the National Weather Service using station KAGC and upstream Allegheny River discharge from the USGS Natrona gauge, without sending member information. Planning sends pickup/start/destination locations and date/time settings to TomTom for geocoding and routing, without names or spreadsheet contents. Expanding routes requests TomTom map tiles for the displayed area; existing road shapes are drawn locally without recalculating routes. These services receive ordinary connection information. TomTom's Privacy Policy applies. Opening navigation links shares the leg's origin and destination with Google Maps under Google's Privacy Policy.

Download your signup Sheets yourself and upload the Excel copies. In the cloud version, Google Apps Script stores these copies and the published plan in the site owner's private Google Drive folder. Only the owner authorizes Drive access; team members use their shared access key. The local version stores files on its private server. Formula text, macros and external workbook links are not executed. Downloaded plan files remain on the devices that saved them. Changing a key or unpublishing a plan cannot recall copies already viewed or downloaded.

The hosting provider may process ordinary request information. Private files and plan API responses use no-store caching. Public images, map tiles and branding may use the ordinary browser cache. Admins should keep server backups private and delete unneeded downloaded files.

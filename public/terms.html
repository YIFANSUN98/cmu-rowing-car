Use of Launch

Launch helps your crew prepare inbound carpool estimates. Review attendance, driver availability, pickup locations, seat capacities and local practice dates before using a plan. Plans based on hypothetical availability are for testing and review.

Estimated pickup and arrival times depend on the supplied inputs and travel estimates. They do not guarantee actual road conditions or arrival times. Follow applicable road rules and exercise driver judgment.

Routing estimates use TomTom and its applicable service terms. Google Maps navigation links are subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and Google Terms of Service. The app's privacy notice explains how planning sends location and timing data to TomTom and opening navigation links sends locations to Google.
